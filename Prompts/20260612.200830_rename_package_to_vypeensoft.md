This plan outlines the steps to rename the package from `com.example` to `com.vypeensoft` across the entire codebase.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The source directory structure will be changed on disk. Files under `app/src/main/java/com/example/apkbackuprestore` will be moved to `app/src/main/java/com/vypeensoft/apkbackuprestore`.
